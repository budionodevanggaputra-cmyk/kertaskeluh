A wrong turn worth exploring. Scroll through an endless photo tunnel and move your pointer to look around.

An interactive 404 page for photographer Per Appelgren. Floating photographs frame the message, while perspective, soft reflections, and animated navigation bring the space to life.

Built with Three.js, custom GLSL shaders, and vanilla JS.
